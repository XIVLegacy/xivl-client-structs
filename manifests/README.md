# Manifest contract

`manifests/` is the machine-readable product of the client-structure catalog.
Files use one of the families below. JSON is UTF-8 with stable formatting;
source citations and raw address strings are data, not disposable comments.

## Naming boundary

Client-side BCS-Y entries preserve source-backed RTTI `Receiver` names.
Opcode-facing prose leads with direction and opcode before any descriptive
packet name.

| Family | Contains | Ownership and regeneration |
|---|---|---|
| Canonical catalogs | `structs.json` and `symbols.json` | Curated BCS-S and BCS-Y source of truth. Struct writes go through `tools/_structs_io.py` and symbol writes go through `tools/_symbols_io.py`; validate with `tools/validate_catalog.py`. |
| Verified datasets | `gam_hash_names.json` | First-party id-to-name resolution over retail-observed hashes. `tools/verify_murmur2.py` verifies every pair and the pinned payload sample coverage. |
| Bridge catalogs | `lua_api_index.json`, receiver and operation maps, `lua_to_opcode.json`, `c2s_bridge_skeleton.json`, `client_class_registry.json`, `receiver_field_writes.json`, `data_dependency_catalog.json`, `lua_apply_chain_firers.json`, and the Control-class N-API maps | Derived views of the canonical catalogs and pinned evidence. Rebuild with the producing commands documented in [`../tools/README.md`](../tools/README.md), then run the catalog gate. Curated receiver and operation overlays are hand-maintained inputs. These catalogs carry embedded `{bcsId, address}` pairs that are denormalized copies of the canonical VA in `symbols.json`; do not hand-edit those addresses, `hygiene_scan.py` A6 gates on their drift. |
| Client-structure IR | `ir_catalog.json` and `ir_overlay.json` | `ir_catalog.json` is generated by `tools/build_ir.py`; `ir_overlay.json` is curated. Run `python tools/build_ir.py` and `python tools/validate_ir.py` after changing an IR input. |
| Frozen snapshots | everything not listed as a named catalog | Evidence records for closed research, frozen as written. The validator classifies by exclusion: `NAMED_CATALOGS` in `tools/hygiene_scan.py` lists named catalogs, and every other `manifests/*.json` is a snapshot. Filenames are topic slugs and serve as identifiers, not descriptions; do not rename, regenerate, or reorganize them casually. Adding a named catalog means adding it to that set. `lua_callsite_context.json` is the preserved-corpus declaration/reference/invocation snapshot for the 19 deferred Lua binding names; regenerate it only with the explicit local script corpus and `tools/extractors/build_lua_callsite_context.py`. |
| RTTI index | `rtti_vftable_index.json` | Generated vftable-VA index. Regenerate it with `tools/build_rtti_index.py` from a local `rtti_extraction_OUR.txt` dump (gitignored, regenerated via `tools/extractors/client_pe`). |

## Catalog value forms

The catalog validator accepts a scalar address such as `0x00D31490`, a
semicolon-joined multi-VA identity, a contiguous `0xAAAAAAAA..0xBBBBBBBB`
range, or the `0x00000000` placeholder. Fixed offsets and sizes use hex
strings or decimal integer strings; offsets may also be `n/a`, `variable`, or
`element+0xNN`, while sizes may carry logical sentinels, Lua-domain
descriptions, or a leading hex value with explanatory prose. These forms carry
meaning for consumers, so a new form must be added to
`tools/validate_catalog.py` and documented here before the validator accepts
it.

Role-refinement rows use the `evidenceKind` vocabulary `pcap_observed`,
`pcap_unobserved`, or `live_validated`. The last value is reserved for a claim
verified by the retail 1.23b client accepting the behavior in a live session.
Canonical struct and symbol entries that depend on a live check may carry
`needsReverify: true` with the required `reverifyMethod` string.

The two IR schemas live in `schemas/`. They are loaded by
`tools/validate_ir.py`; a schema that is not loaded by the gate does not belong
in this repository.
